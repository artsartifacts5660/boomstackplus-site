Inside the Impulso Matrix™

Boom Stack+ Team · 6 min read

"Proprietary blend" is usually a polite way of saying "we won't tell you the doses." We think that's backwards. If an ingredient earns a spot in the can, it earns its dose on the label. Here's everything inside a Boom Stack+ — and why.

The full stack, per 12 oz can

  • Natural Caffeine (Green Coffee) — 150 mg. The engine. Enough to support real alertness; moderate enough to drink daily without living on the edge.
  • L-Theanine — 150 mg. The smoothing agent. Paired 1:1 with caffeine to support calm, focused attention instead of jitters.
  • L-Citrulline — 1,500 mg. An amino acid found in pre-workouts (and almost never in ready-to-drink cans) that supports circulation and muscular endurance.
  • L-Tyrosine — 500 mg. Supports mental performance, especially under stress and demanding work.
  • Taurine — 750 mg. A longtime performance staple that supports exercise capacity and recovery.
  • Potassium Citrate — 200 mg & Sodium — 75 mg. Electrolytes that support hydration and balance, sip after sip.

What's NOT inside

  • No sugar bomb — about 4g cane sugar, supported by monk fruit and stevia (~20 calories).
  • No sucralose, no candy-sweet coating on your tongue.
  • No mystery blend — every dose is printed, every claim maps to an ingredient.
